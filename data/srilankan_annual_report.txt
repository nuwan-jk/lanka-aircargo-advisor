SRILANKAN AIRLINES CARGO - ANNUAL REPORT (EXCERPTS ON CARGO OPERATIONS)

1. Total Cargo Handled (2025): 135,000 Metric Tonnes.
2. Major Export Destinations: London (LHR), Maldives (MLE), Frankfurt (FRA), and Melbourne (MEL).

5. Strategic Initiatives: Fully digitalized e-AWB (Electronic Air Waybill) implementation reached 95% across all network stations.
